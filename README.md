# 🌌 PARALLEL WORLDS: 12 PIXEL DIMENSIONS

> *"12 Pixel Realities. 6 Counterpart Heroes. One Way Home."*

**PARALLEL WORLDS** is a retro, action-packed 2D pixel platformer game built entirely with vanilla **HTML5 Canvas**, **CSS3**, and the **Web Audio API**. Traverse 12 distinct parallel dimensions, master unique physics, survive terrifying enemy types, and vanquish massive mechanical and supernatural bosses to restore order to the multiverse.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🗺️ 12-World Multiverse Campaign
1. **World 1 — Pixel Guardians**: Patrolling bodyguards and alert security drones introducing core jumping & exploration.
2. **World 2 — Cyber Systems**: Futuristic mechs and airborne drones that summon a 2-HP **Mega Mech** when alerted.
3. **World 3 — Dark & Scary**: Shadow ghouls that turn invisible before lunging, rewarding elevated platform safety.
4. **World 4 — Time Citadel (Boss: Kronos)**: Time mafia sentinels leading to **Kronos**, armed with a hydraulic slam fist and precision chronocannon (3s CD).
5. **World 5 — Swamp World**: Sludge environments and **Water Goblins** spitting green toxic projectile bursts (~30s intervals).
6. **World 6 — Red Abyss**: Visceral flesh terrain and **Crimson Flesh Giants** that split into two mini flesh creatures upon defeat.
7. **World 7 — Ancient Sands**: Crumbling desert ruins populated by relentless, slow-moving **Sandy Ghouls**.
8. **World 8 — Graveyard Boss (Boss: Necrowler)**: Crypt arena featuring 7 graves, bone scythe throws (15s), and animated skeleton summoning rituals (45s).
9. **World 9 — Haunted Tundra**: Slippery glacial ice and slow **Ashinagas** launching long-range frost projectiles. *(Part 1 of Glacier unlock)*.
10. **World 10 — Valley of Ruins**: Ancient colosseum structures defended by resilient **2-HP Armored Warriors**. *(Part 2 of Glacier unlock)*.
11. **World 11 — Soulyard Matrix**: Unstable glitch zones and drifting **Static Souls** that pulse screen-blur distortion waves (35s CD).
12. **World 12 — Matrix Godly (Final Boss: Atticus de Helios)**: Grand dual flaming-sword battle that dynamically fractures into **Atticus Ash** (flame cannon mech) and **Helios** (frost core avatar).

### 🕹️ Controls

#### 💻 Desktop / Keyboard
| Action | Key(s) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left / Right** | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` |
| **Jump** | `W`, `Space`, or `↑` |
| **Kronell Ability** | `J` (Chrono Blast) |
| **Glacier Ability** | `K` (Absolute Zero Freeze) |
| **Helios Ability** | `L` (Solar Mini-Sun) |
| **Atticus Ability** | `U` (Summon Ash Phantoms) |
| **Pause Game** | `P` |

#### 📱 Mobile / Touch
- **On-Screen D-Pad (◄ / ►)**: Walk sideways
- **Jump Button (▲)**: Jump over obstacles and stomp enemy heads
- **Ability Button (`POW`)**: Triggers the active hero's ability

---

### 💊 Exploration & Power-Ups
- **Pixel Medkits**: Placed on elevated platforms to restore **+2 HP**.
- **2-Block Stools**: Temporarily gives your hero giant growth boost and extra stomping power.
- **Portal Compass Radar**: Real-time HUD tracking that guides you to each dimension's exit gateway.
