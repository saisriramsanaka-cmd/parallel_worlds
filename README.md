# 🌌 PARALLEL WORLDS: 12 PIXEL DIMENSIONS

> *"12 Pixel Realities. 6 Counterpart Heroes. One Ultimate Multiverse Battle."*

**PARALLEL WORLDS** is a retro, fast-paced 2D pixel platformer built with vanilla **HTML5 Canvas**, **CSS3**, and synthesized audio using the **Web Audio API**. Traverse 12 distinct parallel dimensions, survive unique enemy hazards, master counterpart hero abilities, and defeat mechanical and supernatural titans to liberate reality.

## ✨ Features & Worlds

### 🗺️ 12-World Campaign
* **World 1 — Pixel Guardians**: Heavy security bodyguards and aerial alert drones.
* **World 2 — Cyber Systems**: Surveillance drones that deploy a searching, 2-HP **Mega Mech**.
* **World 3 — Dark & Scary**: Camouflaged wraiths that fade into shadows before striking.
* **World 4 — Time Citadel (Boss: Kronos)**: Mafia-styled chronosentinals leading to **Kronos**, armed with a hydraulic piston fist and rapid chronocannon.
* **World 5 — Swamp World**: Sludge pools and **Water Goblins** spitting green toxic bursts.
* **World 6 — Red Abyss**: Visceral flesh terrain and **Crimson Flesh Giants** that split into twin mini-monsters upon defeat.
* **World 7 — Ancient Sands**: Crumbling ruins defended by resilient **Sandy Ghouls**.
* **World 8 — Graveyard Boss (Boss: Necrowler)**: Crypt arena featuring 7 graves, bone scythe throws, and animated skeleton summoning rituals.
* **World 9 — Haunted Tundra**: Low-friction ice platforms and frost-launching **Ashinagas** *(Part 1 of Glacier Unlock)*.
* **World 10 — Valley of Ruins**: Ancient colosseum paths guarded by **2-HP Armored Warriors** *(Part 2 of Glacier Unlock)*.
* **World 11 — Soulyard Matrix**: Glitch-heavy dimension with **Static Souls** pulsing screen-distortion waves.
* **World 12 — Matrix Godly (Final Boss: Atticus de Helios)**: Dual flaming-sword boss that fractures into **Atticus Ash** (flame cannon mech) and **Helios** (glacial frost core).

### 👑 Creator Access Mode
* Access via the **"CREATOR ONLY 👑"** button on the title screen.
* **Passcode:** `sss!`
* **Perks:**
  * Instantly unlocks all 12 Worlds and all 6 Heroes.
  * **Disables all ability cooldowns & use limits** (infinite Time Orbs, Phantoms, Glacial Freezes, and Mini Suns).
  * Runs entirely in session memory (RAM) with no permanent disk writes.

### 🕹️ Controls

**Desktop / Keyboard**
* **Move:** `A` / `D` or `←` / `→`
* **Jump:** `W`, `Space`, or `↑`
* **Kronell Ability:** `J` (Chrono Blast)
* **Glacier Ability:** `K` (Absolute Zero Freeze)
* **Helios Ability:** `L` (Solar Mini-Sun)
* **Atticus Ability:** `U` (Summon Ash Phantoms)
* **Pause:** `P`

**Mobile / Touch**
* **On-Screen D-Pad (◄ / ►):** Horizontal movement
* **Jump Button (▲):** Leap across platform gaps and stomp enemies
* **Ability Button (`POW`):** Activates active hero power

---

### 💊 Exploration & Pickups
* **Pixel Medkits:** Found on elevated platforms to restore **+2 HP**.
* **2-Block Stools:** Grants temporary size growth and increased stomping power.
* **Portal Compass:** Dynamic HUD radar tracking the direction and distance to the world exit.
